Here's the thing: 2026 has been a big year for multifamily consolidation. Independence Realty Trust and Centerspace just announced an $8.1 billion merger. Equity Residential and AvalonBay already combined into Vivmark Residential. BentallGreenOak has a pending deal to acquire Bell Partners.

Every one of these deals creates the same problem on the ground:

- Two brands, two resident experiences, one platform that has to make it feel like a single company
- A press release that goes out in a day, and 18 months of actual integration work that follows it
- Local community identity that's easy to lose the moment everything gets folded into one national platform

Sound familiar if you're sitting on either side of one of these deals? The risk was never the merger itself. It's losing what made residents feel like they belonged somewhere, right when you most need them to stay.

Let's break it down. If I were running digital for a newly merged multifamily platform, here's what I'd be asking:

- Which local brand equity is actually worth keeping, and which isn't
- How to unify the backend, leasing, reporting, resident communication, without flattening the resident experience on the front end
- How to tell residents about the change before it reads like a takeover

This is exactly the kind of work an integrated strategy, creative, and technology team should be doing alongside the deal team, not six months after close.
