## Draft 1 — Data / Commentary Take (Multifamily)

**Hook:**
41% of apartment communities are now offering concessions. That's not a leasing problem. It's a differentiation problem.

**Body:**
The average multifamily concession just hit 11.1%, the deepest discount in over 25 years. National vacancy is above 7%. And 41.2% of properties are now competing on price, up nearly 10 points year over year.

Here's the thing about a discount arms race: everyone loses the same way. When four out of ten of your competitors are cutting rent to fill units, the discount stops being a differentiator. It's the price of entry.

The communities that win the next 12 months won't be the ones with the deepest concession. They'll be the ones renters already remember before they start their search — a brand, a story, a reason to tour that has nothing to do with a free month.

Concessions get you the tour. Brand gets you the lease.

This is the conversation we're having with every multifamily partner right now: where's the ceiling on price competition, and where do you start building pricing power back.

## Draft 3 — Quick Take / POV (MPC)

**Hook:**
The new-home market is down 5.6% this year. Master-planned communities are up 3%. Same housing market, opposite result.

**Body:**
Top 50 MPCs grew sales at mid-year while the broader new-home market fell. Florida and Texas are leading the way, with The Villages, Lakewood Ranch, Wellen Park, and Babcock Ranch topping the list.

Our take: this isn't luck. When affordability gets tight, buyers get more risk-averse — and a well-placemade community reduces perceived risk in a way a subdivision on a spec lot never will. Amenities, lifestyle branding, a real sense of what daily life looks like there — that lowers the psychological bar to writing the biggest check of someone's life.

Rates aren't the only lever left. Placemaking is the one MPC developers can actually control, and right now it's the one paying off.

**Signal source:** External Signals #2 — RCLCO 2026 Mid-Year Master-Planned Communities Report (score 19/20, MPC).
